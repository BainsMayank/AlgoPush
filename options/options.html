AlgoPush Configuration

Getting Started

  1. Click Connect GitHub Account below, approve on GitHub, and pick or create a repo.
  2. Click Connect Codeforces Account to verify your handle via Codeforces' own login.
  3. Click Connect LeetCode Account to confirm your LeetCode username from your signed-in session.
  4. Click Connect AtCoder Account to confirm your AtCoder handle from your signed-in session.
  5. Click Connect CodeChef Account to confirm your CodeChef username from your signed-in session.
  6. Click Sync Old Submissions to import everything you've already solved.
  7. Solve a new problem to confirm live sync works — it pushes automatically the moment a submission is Accepted.

Codeforces Account

Verifies which Codeforces handle you own via Codeforces' own OpenID Connect login — your Codeforces password never touches this extension. This only confirms your identity; AlgoPush still reads your submissions from your normal logged-in browser session.

LeetCode Account

Confirms which LeetCode account this browser is signed in as, asked of LeetCode directly — your LeetCode password never touches this extension. LeetCode publishes no OAuth login for third-party apps, so there is no redirect to approve: click Connect and it verifies your signed-in session. If you are not signed in yet, LeetCode's own sign-in page opens and the connection completes by itself the moment you finish.

Platforms

Historical Sync

Imports everything you've already solved, using your connections above — nothing to type in. The Codeforces import opens a single background tab on codeforces.com and reads each solution through the same request the site's own “source” button uses, so no extra tabs appear and nothing is hammered. If Codeforces runs a verification check, the sync pauses, clears it (bringing the tab forward only if it needs you), and carries on. The LeetCode import works the same way, through your signed-in leetcode.com session. The AtCoder import lists what you have solved from the public AtCoder Problems dataset — which is also where the difficulty estimates your solutions are filed under come from, since AtCoder publishes neither — and then reads each solution from your own signed-in atcoder.jp session. The CodeChef import walks your own submission history from your signed-in codechef.com session and reads each solution through the same endpoint the site's own “View solution” page uses. All four runs are resumable: anything already pushed is skipped, so you can stop and re-run any time, and the solution index at the root of your repo is written once per run rather than after every submission.







Privacy

Clears your GitHub, Codeforces, LeetCode, AtCoder, and CodeChef connections and local sync history from this browser. This does not delete anything already pushed to GitHub, and does not uninstall the GitHub App (do that from GitHub's own settings).
